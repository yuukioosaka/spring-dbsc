This page must be served over HTTPS. If your browser shows a certificate warning, accept it once — the DBSC cookies are __Host- prefixed and Secure, so they are invisible over plain HTTP and the flow cannot be exercised at all.

Sign in with demo / demo. On success the server calls dbsc.bind() with the servlet session id, which sets Secure-Session-Registration plus two short-lived cookies.

Chromium 145+ then POSTs /dbsc/registration on its own within about a second — no JavaScript involved. Watch the network tab for it.
